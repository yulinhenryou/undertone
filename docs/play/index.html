Interactive prototype

Join the chorus, a little at a time.

A song has started in your karaoke room. Your watch brings your voice in step by step, from listening to singing beside the lead, and back out whenever you like.

Try it

  1. Enter the room, confirm your seat
  2. Turn the wheel once to join softly
  3. Keep turning, all the way to Duet
  4. Make the room loud
  5. Leave: turn down, or tap Turn off
Scroll on the watch, drag the side wheel, swipe the screen, or press ↑ ↓.
